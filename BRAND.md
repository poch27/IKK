# IKK — Brand Context

## What IKK means

**IKK** means **Ink Knit Kult**.

- **Ink** — the printed message, type, and graphic language on each piece.
- **Knit** — the garment itself: considered, wearable, and made to be lived in.
- **Kult** — a community built through shared references; the people who recognize the line, recognize each other.

“I Know Kung-fu” is the first important expression of IKK—not the meaning of the name. Its coded typography and *Matrix* reference established the visual attitude for the brand: quiet at first glance, meaningful to the people who know.

## Brand idea

IKK is a clothing brand built around memorable movie lines. Rather than treating quotes as simple references, each design should turn a line into a wearable attitude: confident, a little cryptic, culturally aware, and clean enough to work beyond fandom.

The goal is for someone to recognize the energy of a quote even when they do not immediately recognize the movie.

## Voice and visual direction

- Cinematic, minimal, and self-assured
- Clever without being overly literal
- Nostalgic references with modern streetwear restraint
- Designs that feel like an inside reference, not merchandise

## Brand colours

The core IKK palette comes from the original black-and-green shirt concept. It should feel dark, tonal, and almost hidden—not bright neon.

| Colour | Suggested digital value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ink Black** | `#0B0D0C` | Primary garments, background, and the dominant visual field |
| **Kult Green** | `#005C35` | IKK mark, typography, small print details, and digital accents |
| **Off White** | `#F5F3EE` | Product-mockup background and restrained contrast when needed |

Use **Kult Green** sparingly against **Ink Black**. The green should read as a deep emerald ink in real life, never a luminous or fluorescent green. Always approve physical print swatches before production; fabric and ink can shift the final tone.

## First collection direction: The Matrix

*The Matrix* is a strong first collection world for IKK. Its ideas—identity, control, belief, choice, and awakening—fit the brand’s coded, insider-reference approach.

### Initial shirt concepts

| Design line | Core feeling |
| --- | --- |
| **Down the Rabbit Hole** | Curiosity, choosing the unknown, going deeper |
| **Dodge This Bullet** | Defiance, precision, confidence under pressure |
| **Free Your Mind** | Breaking limits and changing perspective |
| **Ignorance Is Bliss** | Comfortable denial, irony, choosing not to know |

## Creative guardrails

- Prioritize the feeling behind a quote over obvious character imagery or scene recreations.
- Keep the IKK mark and typography versatile across shirts, tags, packaging, and digital drops.
- Build each release around a clear cinematic idea so the clothing has a story, not just a printed line.
- Use original artwork and interpretations; avoid using copyrighted film stills, logos, actor likenesses, or exact studio graphics without permission.
